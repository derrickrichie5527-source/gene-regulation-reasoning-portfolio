# Simulated data provenance

All values were constructed for this case. Panel D is generated as 2^(-t/4) and 2^(-t/6), rounded to six decimals. Other panels are deliberately selected to illustrate normalization, recovery and synthesis-blockade problems. No replicate count, error bar, biological significance or patient origin is implied. Panel D controls and steady-state conditions are stipulated scenario assumptions, not controls performed by the owner.
