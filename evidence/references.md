# References and source scope

Research checked on 8 October 2026. Numerical values in the case are original simulated teaching values, not extracted from these studies. No source establishes a mechanism for fictional compound X or protein R.

## S1

[Bustin et al. (2025). MIQE 2.0: Revision of the Minimum Information for Publication of Quantitative Real-Time PCR Experiments Guidelines. Clinical Chemistry 71:634-651.](https://academic.oup.com/clinchem/article/71/6/634/8119148)

Peer-reviewed methodological consensus. Reference validation, amplification efficiency, normalization and reporting. Publisher search-index content accessed; direct page retrieval was intermittent.

## S2

[Russo et al. (2017). Metabolic Labeling and Recovery of Nascent RNA to Accurately Quantify mRNA Stability. Methods 120:39-48.](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC5447484/)

Peer-reviewed methods article. Recovery spike controls, labeling incorporation and perturbation. Full-text methods inspected.

## S3

[Taylor and Posch (2014). The Design of a Quantitative Western Blot Experiment. BioMed Research International 2014:361590.](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC3971489/)

Peer-reviewed methodological article with assay demonstrations. Linear range, antibody specificity and normalization. Full-text workflow inspected; authors were affiliated with an assay vendor.

## S4

[Oh, Chen and Varshavsky (2017). A reference-based protein degradation assay without global translation inhibitors. Journal of Biological Chemistry 292:21457-21465.](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC5766948/)

Peer-reviewed original research in yeast. Demonstrates a specific translation-inhibitor artifact; does not establish its magnitude in human cells or fictional protein R. Full-text results inspected.

## S5

[Fierro-Monti et al. (2013). A Novel Pulse-Chase SILAC Strategy Measures Changes in Protein Decay and Synthesis Rates Induced by Perturbation of Proteostasis with an Hsp90 Inhibitor. PLOS ONE 8:e80423.](https://journals.plos.org/plosone/article?id=10.1371/journal.pone.0080423)

Peer-reviewed original research in human cells. Protein synthesis/decay modeling and correction for growth dilution. Publisher equations and interpretation inspected.

## S6

[Maximum entropy determination of mammalian proteome dynamics (2024). Proceedings of the National Academy of Sciences. DOI: 10.1073/pnas.2313107121.](https://www.pnas.org/doi/10.1073/pnas.2313107121)

Peer-reviewed original research. First-order turnover, half-life and steady-state relationships; pulse-chase assumptions. Publisher article and indexed equation text inspected.

## S7

[Fulco et al. (2016). Systematic mapping of functional enhancer-promoter connections with CRISPR interference. Science 354:769-773.](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC5438575/)

Peer-reviewed original research. Functional perturbation of endogenous regulatory elements. Indexed full-text results and PubMed record inspected. Supports the approach, not a specific element or direct compound target in this case.
